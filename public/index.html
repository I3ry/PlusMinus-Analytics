THE CASE STUDY

From a dashboard to a product story.

The problem

Shot totals show activity, but a developing player still needs to decide what to practice. The hypothesis: visible evidence and a simple next step make analytics more useful.

The tradeoff

Validate the decision loop with repeatable data and transparent rules first. Camera tracking and AI explanations remain gated roadmap items.

The result

A runnable prototype and reviewable product specification. Technical feasibility is demonstrated at prototype scale; adoption and performance improvement are not yet measured.
